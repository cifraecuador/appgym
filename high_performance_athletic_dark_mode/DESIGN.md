## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered specifically for premium athletic facility managers, gym owners, and high-performance club administrators. The emotional tone delivers commanding focus, physical discipline, and real-time operational control. The aesthetic merges **High-Contrast / Bold** principles with refined **Dark Mode Glassmorphism**, projecting raw athletic power combined with executive financial precision.

Key attributes:
- **Atmospheric & Immersive:** Pure OLED black foundations paired with progressive carbon-slate container surfaces, eliminating visual noise and highlighting operational metrics.
- **Electric Precision:** High-voltage neon lime drives actionable CTAs, progression meters, and positive performance states.
- **Critical Urgency:** High-impact blaze orange commands immediate attention for expired memberships, retention drop-offs, and payment lapses.
- **Data Muscle:** Bold metrics, condensed progress rings, and tactile cards evoke high-end fitness tracking hardware translated into B2B mobile management.

## Colors

The palette establishes an aggressive, high-contrast dark environment designed for readability under fluctuating gym lighting conditions.

### Primary & Secondary Accents
- **Primary Electric Lime (`#D4FF00`):** Reserved for primary interactive elements, positive trends, active status indicators, and hero conversion touchpoints. In dark surfaces, it offers maximum contrast. Typography on top of primary fills must strictly use `#0D0D12`.
- **Secondary Blaze Orange (`#FF5C00`):** Dictates urgent administrative actions, imminent membership expirations (1–3 days), payment defaults, and critical member alerts.
- **Tertiary Slate Carbon (`#2A2A38`):** Serves as interactive secondary fills, elevated structural badges, and divider anchors.

### Neutral System & Layering
- **`surface-base` (`#0D0D12`):** Primary canvas background on OLED/AMOLED displays, saving battery and maximizing depth.
- **`surface-card` (`#16161E`):** Standard container surface for member cards, KPI blocks, and interactive widgets.
- **`surface-raised` (`#1F1F2C`):** Modals, bottom navigation sheets, floating pills, and elevated headers.
- **`text-primary` (`#FFFFFF`):** High-impact headlines, critical numeric values, and direct labels.
- **`text-secondary` (`#A0A0B2`):** Contextual metadata, plan subtitles, timestamps, and descriptive hints.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels to combine geometric clarity with contemporary sport aesthetic. 

- **Numerical Hierarchy:** Primary business metrics (MRR, active memberships, churn ratios, revenue totals) use `metric-xl` or `metric-md` with weights `700` and `800`. Negative letter spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) produces an aggressive, compact visual profile.
- **Headings & Titles:** Screen titles and major section dividers use `ExtraBold` (`800`) or `Bold` (`700`) to create instant structural orientation.
- **Body & Captions:** `body-md` and `body-sm` default to `400` (Regular) and `500` (Medium) on secondary text (`#A0A0B2`), maintaining high legibility on dark surfaces without causing optical halation.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Micro badges, chip headers, and table headers utilize uppercase or tracked weights with `0.02em` to `0.04em` tracking to preserve legibility at 10px–12px.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for native mobile screens (iOS & Android) with a 4px/8px base rhythm.

### Grid & Margins
- **Screen Canvas Margin:** Fixed `1.25rem` (20px) horizontal padding across mobile viewports, providing thumb clearance while maximizing content density.
- **Internal Gutters:** `1rem` (16px) separation between cards, multi-column metric tiles, and operational modules.
- **Compact Spacing:** Micro gaps inside list items and metric chips use `0.25rem` (4px) and `0.5rem` (8px).

### Responsive Viewports
- **Compact Mobile (<390px):** Metric pairs reflow into vertical stack cards; outer margins compress to `1rem` (16px).
- **Standard Mobile (390px - 430px):** 2-column KPI blocks, side-by-side quick actions, standard `1.25rem` outer margins.
- **Tablet / Large Mobile Foldables (>600px):** 4-column KPI overview, master-detail split list for member directories and direct profile inspections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on traditional drop shadows; instead, it uses **tonal luminance stepping**, **radial back-lighting**, and **delicate translucent borders**.

1. **Base Tier (Ground):** `#0D0D12` base canvas with optional subtle atmospheric radial gradients (`radial-gradient(ellipse at top, rgba(212, 255, 0, 0.04), transparent 60%)`).
2. **Card Tier (Structural Containers):** `#16161E` background paired with a `1px` outer stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` or `#2A2A38`.
3. **Elevated Tier (Interactive Modals & Menus):** `#1F1F2C` surface with a diffuse ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55), 0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`.
4. **Neon Glow Accentuation:** Selected pills, active indicators, and high-priority action buttons project a soft tinted aura (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(212, 255, 0, 0.25)`).
5. **Urgent Hazard Tier:** Warning cards and imminent expiration pills emit a localized amber-orange aura (`box-shadow: 0 4px 18px rgba(255, 92, 0, 0.2)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape identity tailored for a modern, tactile mobile experience:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) radius.
- **Primary Content Cards & Metric Tiles:** `1rem` (16px) to `1.25rem` (20px) radius.
- **Modal Sheets & Hero Feature Panels:** `1.5rem` (24px) top corner radius.
- **Status Badges, Filter Chips & Icon Pills:** Fully rounded / pill-shaped (`9999px`) to maintain instant cognitive distinction from rectangular cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#D4FF00`, text `#0D0D12` (`Plus Jakarta Sans 700`), min-height 52px, corner radius 16px. Tap state features slight scale down (`scale: 0.98`) with luminous spread.
- **Secondary / Actionable Neutral:** Background `#1F1F2C`, border `1px solid #2A2A38`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover/pressed background `#28283A`.
- **Destructive / Urgent Action:** Background `rgba(255, 92, 0, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 92, 0, 0.35)`, text `#FF5C00`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Active / Paid Badge:** Pill shape (`9999px`), background `rgba(212, 255, 0, 0.12)`, text `#D4FF00`, border `1px solid rgba(212, 255, 0, 0.25)`.
- **Expiring Soon (1–7 days):** Background `rgba(255, 92, 0, 0.12)`, text `#FF5C00`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 92, 0, 0.3)`.
- **Expired / Inactive:** Background `rgba(160, 160, 178, 0.1)`, text `#A0A0B2`, border `1px solid rgba(160, 160, 178, 0.2)`.
- **Filter Chips (Directorio Dinámico):** Unselected state `#16161E` with `#A0A0B2` text; selected state features filled `#D4FF00` or `#1F1F2C` with high-contrast text and a subtle glow.

### Cards & Member Tiles
- **Member Tile:** Height 76px–84px, `#16161E` background, 16px radius, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` border. Left avatar with circular border status ring (Green = Active, Orange = Expirando). Right side contains quick action triggers (e.g., green WhatsApp icon button or Orange Notification trigger).
- **Financial Metric Card:** `#16161E` surface, featuring a top metric label (`label-sm`), large monetary figure (`metric-xl`), and micro-trend badge with directional icon (`+14.2%` in `#D4FF00`).

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Search Bar:** Height 48px, background `#16161E`, border `1px solid #2A2A38`, radius 14px, leading search icon in `#A0A0B2`. Focus state shifts border to `#D4FF00` with an outer ring of `rgba(212, 255, 0, 0.15)`.

### Lists & Navigation
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:** Floating dock or anchored bar with `#16161E` surface, backdrop-filter blur `20px`, top edge stroke `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Active icons illuminate in `#D4FF00` with a 4px under-dot.
- **Empty States:** Centered 48px muted iconography in `#2A2A38`, header in `#FFFFFF` (`headline-sm`), body text in `#A0A0B2` (`body-sm`), followed by a compact Primary CTA button.
